Extracurriculars

Clubs, competitions, and other involvement outside the classroom at Texas A&M.

Involvement

Aggie Coding Club

August 2024 – Present

A student club for writing code and building projects outside of any class requirement, through casual meetups, workshops, and challenges.

Impact:A place to code for the fun of it instead of a grade — kept me sharp on skills that don't always come up in coursework and connected me with other students who like to build things.

IEEE

August 2025 – Present

The student branch of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers — talks, industry connections, and professional development for electrical and computer engineers.

Impact:A more professional, industry-facing counterpart to my coursework — a way to see where the ECE degree actually leads and to network with people already working in the field.

TAMU Datathon

April 2024 – Present

A recurring campus hackathon/datathon I've kept coming back to as a competitor, most recently taking 1st place in its submission category with the Solana MCP Server.

Impact:Repeated exposure to building something real under a hard deadline — the event that's produced some of the projects I'm most proud of, including a 1st-place finish.

Pickleball Club

August 2024 – Present

A casual, social sports club for playing pickleball on a regular basis with other students.

Impact:A reliable way to step away from screens and coursework, stay active, and build friendships that have nothing to do with engineering.

The Big Event

September 2024

Texas A&M's student-run day of community service, where thousands of Aggies volunteer around Bryan/College Station to say "thank you" to the local community.

Impact:A grounding reminder that the university sits inside a real community — a day of manual work for someone else's benefit instead of my own résumé.

Interests

Hardware Restoration for Retro Consoles

Repairing and restoring old game consoles and handhelds — recapping boards, cleaning contacts, replacing shells and screens, and diagnosing dead units.

Impact:Hands-on soldering and hardware debugging that directly reinforces what I study in my ECE classes — and honestly, the whole reason this site is styled like a Game Boy.

Computer Building

Planning, sourcing parts for, and building PCs from scratch — balancing budget, compatibility, and performance.

Impact:Built an intuitive, practical understanding of how the components in a computer architecture course actually come together into a working machine.

Board Games

Playing strategy and tabletop games with friends, from quick card games to longer, more involved board games.

Impact:A low-stakes way to practice strategic thinking and planning ahead, and a reliable excuse to get friends in the same room.

Gym

Regular strength training and exercise.

Impact:Gives structure to the week and an outlet for stress — the discipline of showing up consistently carries over directly into how I handle coursework and deadlines.
